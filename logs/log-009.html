MISSION // SE-2026

009 - Unexpected Ramping

[2026-06-11 17:32 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

While the traditional nights for relaxation and connection between terra-dwellers is typically labeled archaic words such as 'Friday', 'Saturday', and 'Sunday', our mission embarked early, on a solar period deemed 'Thursday'. Embarkation was timed to eclipse an Hour of Happiness at the Nomad Star Cluster followed by the floating Antone pod at a hydrogen distillation plant. Coordination was planned for our chiefs of communication to rendez-vous at the plant post star cluster. We didn't anticipate another inhabitable star in the cluster - named after the infamous Kelly of I-cluster 49798. Encounters of other explorers, including one with leg damage (twice-encountered and approached by chief navigations officer), one who had recently survived a fight in KI49798 (approached chief navigations officer), a solo female frequenter of KI49798, and the staff. The diverse and interesting locale of KI49798 led to miscalibration of time equipment and the captain's tardiness for the main rendez-vous. The hydgrogen distillation plant was much too loud, and we retreated to a nearby warehouse. Smooch 001 was obtained.

"Time calibration is a tricky thing, best not to be tampered with."
Transmission Over
