MISSION // SE-2026

006 - Asteroid Collision

[2026-06-06 20:58 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

Captain and Chief Navigations Officer struck out to new coordinates. Exploration included a locations where the terra-dwellers appreciate sport (a curious game with thickened players swinging sticks to fling a small, black object across ice), and a recommended eatery of small nutritional fare and cultivated vegetation. The third location met with mixed results, where the following incident can be attributed to the wayward actions of the chief navigations officer. The location offered a small ecosystem of 'dive' terra culture, which the exploration crew observed until radar detected an approaching mass from nearby orbit. This came to a surprise to the captain, but not to the navigation officer, for while the crew's course was set, it was transmitted to air-space where it was vulnerable for interception, and received by the honing oribital mass. The captain noticed environmental lights in the dive ecosystem flashed, and while they were for medical attention to an over-indulged terra-dweller, they may as well have warned the crew for impact. At time of impact, a terra-dweller explained to the captain that it is a common occurance for people in deep space to fall into spacial disorientation, curated amnesia, and illogical behavior. The terra sage warned that the emptiness of space is lonely, and we often forget the impact of asteroids and previous lovers. The ship requires inspection. Damage unknown.

"First asteroid - shame on it. Second asteroid - shame on me."
