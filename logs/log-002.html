MISSION // SE-2087

Log 002 — Departure from Lunar Gateway

[2026-06-01 02:12 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

It is truly interesting to watch the locals interact in their natural environments. The natives of this planet enjoy a wide variety of entertainment surrounding drinks and music. From music that is imported from differing land masses, to sounds that are blasted from large speakers, controlled by an expert at their control center, me and my navigation officer and chief medical officer witnessed the range of experience.

The styles of the local population did not differ much, and I was afraid that my choice in celebratory costume was too strange. My garb included a long skirt and green shadows about my eyes, and perhaps next time I will not color my eyes like this and wear shorter materials.

After enjoying a feast from a desert land, The Chief Medical Officer had connections amongst a group of, primarily male, revelers. While none approached me, I observed as local women approached the men. The practices here are indeed strange.

Systems confirmed at low fuel reserves, but safely disengaged to ship. Navigation officer successfully located, Chief Medical Officer location is unknown.

"A rooftop beneath the stars is nothing if there are no stars in your eyes."
