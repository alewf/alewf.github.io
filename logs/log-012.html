MISSION // SE-2026

012 - Uncovering A New Class of Terra-Dweller

[2026-06-20 02:37 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

A string of unexpected events spun the adventuring pod to new frontiers at the finale of this solar week. The expedition began with a nutrient acquisition, adding a 'burger' to the energy stores. Notably in this expedition, a new class of terra-dweller appeared to the crew. They speak differently, with lilting sounds, and are just as fascinated with us as we are with them. At a terra-wide sporting spectacle, the new terra-dweller class appeared in droves, self-identifying with coordinated colors, like this planet's venomous species. With an evenly application of chemical compounds, the mission's crew uncovered a black hole, opening to a parallel dimension extending past midnight. In this black hole, the terra-dwellers spoke easily and approached frequently. A rotating cast of characters appeared, from places such as: Michigan, Indiana, Manchester, Scotland, Atlanta, London, Australia, Connecticut. Billiards, a practice well-known to this crew, was exercised at two locales. At a third, the captain wrangled a space automaton to the captain's later chagrin. A second nutrient acquisition was required to preventatively protect energy stores. After escorting a visiting class of terra-dwellers through the main avenue of the black hole where the remnants of the Friday swarms dwindled, the captian and chief navigations officer acquired Smooch #2 and Smooch #3. The rip in the space-time continuum was appropriately contained until our departure.

"The robot cow won't ride itself."
