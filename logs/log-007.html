Log 007 — Broadband Signal Communications Decoded

[2026-06-07 18:32 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

The terra-dwellers release entertainment signals. Such signals form a video broadcast of fictional romances. Is there an ulterior motive behind these transmissions? I have taken it upon myself for the sake of the crew to analyze this signal with the aid of a full bottle of fermented grapes - a terra delicacy, often consumed ritualistically for viewing purposes. Damage was immediate. Electrical pulses delievered to my neuroreceptors translated to unexpected joy, yet this stimulation could not be sustained. Post-transmission, the brain relapses to its previous empty state yet weakened. Leading to inactivity and ultimate desolation of the neural pathways. Unfortunately, such a pattern is addicting in nature. I shall warn the crew.

"The poison of the contemporary age will be psycho-electrical."
Transmission Over
