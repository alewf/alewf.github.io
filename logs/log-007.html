MISSION // SE-2026

006 - Asteroid Collision

[2026-06-06 20:58 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

On this particular mission, the captain and chief navigations officer struck out alone to new coordinates. A successful stop to a location where the terra-dwellers appreciate sports (a sport where thickened players use sticks to toss around a small, black object on ice), and then a well-recommended location of small eats and cultivated vegetation. The third location met with mixed results. For while, the location offered a small ecosystem of 'dive' terra culture, we were soon not alone. The following incidents can be attributed to the wayward actions of the chief navigations officer. A terra-dweller explained to me at the sidebar that it is a common occurance for people in deep space to occur. The emptiness of space is lonely, we forget the impact of asteroids and previous lovers. For while our course was set, it was transmitted to air-space where it was received, leaving us vulnerable for interception. Lights flashed, and while they were for medical attention to an over-indulged terra-dweller, they may as well have warned the crew for impact. The ship requires inspection. Damage unknown.

"First asteroid - shame on it. Second asteroid - shame on me."
