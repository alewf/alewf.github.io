MISSION // SE-2026

010 - Exploration of New Coordinates

[2026-06-12 23:59 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

After the unexpected ramping of a non-designated intake period, the crew faced a quick turn-around time for the following mission. Communications for weekend exploration mission had been received from unsecured comminication channels. The received coordinates led to unknown territory deep in the terra hills. A shipwreck, nestled amongst the vegetation offered us refurge past midnight. The galley was well-stocked, and the crew hospitable. The shipwrecked captain wandered the halls, as though examining the glory of a full deck. Music was provided by a list of artists, the main hydrogen pools were filled with inflated serpents for entertainment. The captain and chief navigation officer took full advantage of the quarters, and partook in the ritualistic rhythm movements encouraged by the curated music. We recognized a crew, from previous explorations, otherwise the terra-dwellers were strange to us, and wore varying shells of color and shape. The previous crew had a spectacular collection of space rocks, dust, and excavation equipment. After dislodging from given coordinates, a brief excusion to PTertel58b resulted in an impromptu refilling of fuel tanks to end the mission.

"We are all made of star dust."
Transmission Over
