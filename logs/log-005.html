MISSION // SE-2026

005 — Terra Sample Obtained

[2026-06-05 17:26 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

While had known that a sample of Terra would need to be obtained for our mission, we had not anticipated such perfect conditions for the extraction. After a solar week of rain, the day of the extraction was no different. While locals gathered to watch a regional tournament of sports (consisting of human specimens in the 99.9th percentile of height, running around an enclosure with an inflated ball), my loyal crew and I gathered the necessary materials. We acquired plastic bags and biodegradable soap, and located our site - a public terrain open to scientists such as ourselves. The mission was a great success. I will venture to say that its success was enhanced by our sense of daring, for certainly, I saw no other crews in the park after dark, just a lone man engaging in steady exercise. And while the crew found great serendipity in such an isolated excursion, it causes me to wonder of the engagement of the locals. While these logs have already noted the conformity amongst the young population, I had not realized the extent of its grip. A practice such as terra abstraction is not illegal when done properly, and yet there are very few earthlings who have done such an activity. While the muddy earth is in high supply, perhaps the same cannot be said of its inhabitants' creativity and willpower.

"This planet is a playground of physics and the imagination."
Transmission Over
