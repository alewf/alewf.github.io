MISSION // SE-2026

011 - Recovery Period and Nutritional Acquisition

[2026-06-14 17:00 UTC] COORD: X:129.22 Y:88.71 Z:450.12

The series of exploration missions officially closed after a period of recovery and the acquisition of nutrients. The captain views the successful avoidance of a nearby asteroid with five-dollar musical cover, mapping and exploration of new deep Terra-hill coordinates, contact and exchange of a space academy representative, leading to the log of anti-gravity spectacles at a cement parkscape, and ending at a well-traveled and highly coveted nutrient acquisition site. While the nutrients were delicious, a few details remain with the captain. A hydrogen contamination and explosion at a neighboring structure, was unmanaged at the time of nutrient acquisition. Overload of nutrients led for the captain, chief navigations officer, and chief medical officer to walk the stretch of bridge past other intake sites. An unplanned stop at a libations intake site, led to the viewing of two notable spectacles. One being the finals of the ice-stick tournament (previously mentioned in log-005). One being an event of apparent social and cultural consequence as determined by its location, its location being the houselawn of the terra-dwellers' executive leader. The event was confounding to the crew, with much violence and fanfare. The terra-dwellers are a blood-thirsty species.

"All divides can be united through fanfare and bloodshed."
